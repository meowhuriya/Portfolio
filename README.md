## ✏️ 100% Fully Editable (Update Everything in Real-Time)

You can customize and update every single section directly from the website without writing any code:

1. **Profile & Bio**:
   - Update your **Full Name**, **Avatar Monogram** (or custom photo image URL), **Role Badge**, **Headline Tagline**, **College Name**, **Batch Period**, and **Meta Badges**.
   - Customize your multi-paragraph **About / Bio** section.
2. **Systems Research & Academic Matrix**:
   - Split into **Foundation Engineering** (`Discrete Mathematics`, `Linear Algebra & Matrix Decompositions`, `Low-Level C Systems Architecture`) and **Independent Research Syllabus** (`Computational Biology` cellular modeling and `Neural Dynamics` bio-signals & edge constraints).
   - Dynamic Status Badges: `In Progress • Semester 1`, `Research Trainee Track`, `First-Principles Study`.
   - Update degree, institution, and tracks seamlessly via the **"✏️ Edit Matrix"** modal.
3. **Technical Arsenal by Layer (4-Tier Systems Architecture)**:
   - Categorized by compute and architecture layers:
     - **Layer 01 - Systems & Core Languages**: `C`, `Python (NumPy)`, `SQL (PostgreSQL)`.
     - **Layer 02 - Data Pipelines & Telemetry**: `Supabase`, `SQLite (Local-First Edge Caching)`, `Delta Sync Protocols`, `REST APIs`.
     - **Layer 03 - Frontier & Applied Compute**: `Computer Vision OCR Parsing`, `Acoustic Triage Processing`, `Offline-First Distributed Logic`.
     - **Layer 04 - Tooling & Environments**: `Git/GitHub Actions`, `Vercel Edge Networks`, `Linux/UNIX environments`.
   - Update all layers with real-time tag rendering via the **"✏️ Edit Layers"** modal.
4. **Current Investigations & Reading Log**:
   - Terminal-styled sub-module tracking active research vectors (`Computational Bio`, `Edge Systems`, `Neuro-Informatics`), specific specs/papers under review, and milestone statuses (`Literature Review`, `Benchmarking In-Engine`, `Prototype Testing`).
   - Fully editable via the **"✏️ Edit Research Log"** modal.
5. **Contact Information**:
   - Update your **Gmail Address**, **LinkedIn Handle**, and **Profile Link**.
6. **Live Deployed Websites Gallery**:
   - Add new projects with name, live URL, description, category, theme gradient, and tech stack tags.
   - Edit or delete any project card.
   - Includes default deployed apps:
     - **Node Binding AI**: [`https://node-binding-ai.vercel.app`](https://node-binding-ai.vercel.app)
     - **PulseAero**: [`https://pulseaero.vercel.app`](https://pulseaero.vercel.app)
7. **Future Ideas & R&D Gallery**:
   - Add, edit, and manage upcoming research ideas, development stages, timelines, and technology stacks.
8. **Backup & Restore (JSON Export / Import)**:
   - Click **"💾 Backup JSON"** to download your complete portfolio configuration as a single `.json` file.
   - Click **"📂 Restore JSON"** to import your backup onto another device or browser.
   - Click **"↺ Reset to Default"** to safely restore the original starting data at any time.
